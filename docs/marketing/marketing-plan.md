## The one-sentence strategy

**Win Bangladeshi university students and fresh graduates first, using Facebook groups, campus clubs and useful career tips, then let search traffic (the new guides and template pages) bring in everyone else.**

Students are the easiest first audience because:

- they all need a CV at the same moments (internship season, final semester, graduation);
- they live in Facebook groups (university, department, club and job groups);
- they share tools with each other when something is free and good;
- campus clubs can bring you hundreds of users at once.

## What makes ResumeX different (use these in every post)

1. **Free PDF, no watermark.** Many resume builders let you design for free and then charge you to download. ResumeX doesn't. This is your strongest hook.
2. **A real ATS check.** It reads the actual PDF like an applicant tracking system and runs 30+ checks, including keyword match against a job description.
3. **50+ designer templates** with any colour and 11 fonts.
4. **Live PDF preview.** What you see is exactly what you download.
5. **Private mode.** Nothing is stored on the servers.
6. **AI help** for bullet points and cover letters (when AI is on). It shows every change for approval and never invents experience.

**After the job-search launch** (only once "V2 workspace for everyone" is on), add:

7. **A CV for every job, in seconds.** Paste the circular; ResumeX picks from your Career Profile. No AI, and nothing is made up.
8. **One board for the whole search:** deadlines, interviews, reminders and the exact CV you sent.
9. **Biodata done privately:** formal biodata templates, with personal details kept in that one CV.

**Never promise jobs** ("guaranteed interview", "get hired in 7 days"). Promise a better CV, faster.

## 3. The 90-day plan

### Days 1–30: Friends, campus and groups (free)
- Launch week above.
- **3 page posts a week** (see the weekly routine).
- **5 to 10 group posts a week** across different groups. Never post the same text twice in the same group.
- Start the **Campus Ambassador programme** at your own university and 2 others.
- Run one **"Free CV review" live or comment thread** per week. People love free feedback, and you'll learn what users struggle with.
- **Target: 500 to 1,000 users.**

### Days 31–60: Campus partnerships and first ads
- Partner with **5 or more university clubs** (career clubs, CSE, BBA, business and debate clubs). Offer a campus code and a 30-minute online "Build your CV in 10 minutes" session.
- Contact **university career services offices** (outreach template included).
- Start **short videos**: 20 to 30 second screen recordings of building a CV or an ATS check before and after. Post them as Facebook Reels (and TikTok/Instagram if you want).
- Try **small Facebook ads** only after you know which organic post works best (see [Paid ads](#paid-ads-only-after-organic-works)).
- Publish **one new guide a month** on the site for SEO (ideas in the SEO checklist).
- **Target: 2,000 to 3,000 users.**

### Days 61–90: Scale what works, prepare to charge
- Double down on the one or two channels that brought the most sign-ups per hour of effort.
- Ask happy users for testimonials and short "my CV before and after" posts (with permission).
- Post a milestone ("1,000 CVs created, thank you!"). Social proof brings more users.
- Decide pricing using real data (see [Making money](#making-money-later)).
- **Target: 4,000 to 5,000 users.**

These targets are guesses to aim for, not promises. Adjust them after the first month.

## Facebook groups: how to post without getting banned

Groups will bring most of your early users, but admins delete ads and ban spammers.

1. **Read the rules first.** Many job groups ban promotions. Ask the admin (template in the outreach file).
2. **Lead with value.** Post a tip, a mini-guide or a free CV review offer, and mention ResumeX at the end. For example: "5 things I learned reviewing 50 fresher CVs… (I built a free tool for this: resumex.cc)".
3. **Don't paste the same text into 20 groups in one day.** Facebook flags it as spam. Do 2 or 3 groups a day, with slightly different text each time.
4. **Answer every comment**, even "nice". Comments push the post up the feed.
5. **Put the link in the first comment** if a group reduces the reach of posts with links, and say "link in comments".

**Types of groups to find** (search Facebook for these):
- your university's official, department, batch and club groups;
- job-posting groups for Bangladesh (search "jobs Bangladesh", "fresher jobs BD", "internship BD");
- career-advice groups (search "career", "CV", "LinkedIn Bangladesh");
- skill groups where people also look for jobs (programming, design, digital marketing, BBA, engineering).

Keep a list in your sheet: group name, size, rules, date posted, result.

## Campus ambassador programme

This is where the **Campaigns** feature in the admin console becomes your best marketing tool.

**How it works**
1. Recruit 1 or 2 students per university (post 19 or B6).
2. Create a campaign code for each university in **Admin → Campaigns**, for example `NSU2026`, `BRACU2026`, `IUB2026`, `DU2026`.
   - Set the plan and credits it unlocks, and for how many days (for example Pro for 60 days).
   - Raise **max uses** from the default of 50 to however many students you expect (for example 500).
   - Optionally limit it to the university's email domain (for example `northsouth.edu`) so only real students can use it.
3. The ambassador shares the join link, `https://resumex.cc/join/NSU2026`, in their groups and clubs.
4. The admin console shows how many people joined with each code. That tells you exactly which campus and which ambassador is working.

**What ambassadors get** (only promise what you'll actually give):
- a certificate and a LinkedIn recommendation from you after 2 months;
- free premium access;
- a small prize for the top ambassador each month (for example a ৳500 to ৳1,000 mobile top-up or bKash transfer), if you can afford it.

**What ambassadors do:** post in their university groups twice a month, share the join link with 2 clubs, and help run one CV session.

## Paid ads (only after organic works)

Don't spend money until:
- the page has 10 or more posts and some real comments;
- you know which post gets the most clicks organically;
- you've watched a few people use the site on a phone without getting stuck.

**First test**
- **Budget:** ৳300 to ৳500 per day for 5 to 7 days (about ৳2,500 to ৳3,500 in total).
- **Objective:** *Traffic*, or *Leads* if you set it up later.
- **Audience:** Bangladesh, ages 19 to 28, interests like job hunting, LinkedIn, Bdjobs, higher education, university students. Let Facebook's "Advantage+" audience expand if results are good.
- **Placements:** Facebook and Instagram feeds and Reels.
- **Creatives:** test 2 or 3 images, such as `03-ats.png`, `01-launch.png` and `09-bangla.png`, with the same text.
- **Landing page:** `https://resumex.cc/ats-checker` for the ATS ad and `https://resumex.cc` for the others.
- Create a separate campaign code or landing page for ads (for example `/join/FBADS`) so you can count sign-ups from ads.

**Rule of thumb:** after 3 days, turn off the creatives with the lowest click-through rate and keep the best one. Work out the cost per sign-up (money spent ÷ new users from the ad), and only increase the budget if it's lower than what a user is worth to you.

**Boosting posts:** the "Boost" button is the simple version. It's fine for a post that is already doing well organically. Boost for ৳200 to ৳300 per day for 3 days.

## Metrics to track weekly

Keep it simple. One row per week in a Google Sheet:

| Week | New users | Resumes created | Page followers | Post reach | Link clicks | Best post | Group posts | Campaign sign-ups (by code) | Ad spend | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

- **New users and resumes:** Admin console → Overview.
- **After the job-search launch:** Admin console → Overview also shows Career Profiles, applications tracked (and this week's) and tailored resumes. Add them to the sheet.
- **Reach and link clicks:** Facebook Page → Insights / Professional dashboard.
- **Campaign sign-ups:** Admin console → Campaigns.
- **Search traffic:** Google Search Console → Performance (after a few weeks).

One number matters most: **new users per week**. If it's growing, keep going. If it's flat for 2 weeks, change the channel or the message, not just the graphic.

**Recommended next step:** add privacy-friendly analytics to the site (for example Plausible, or Google Analytics 4 with a cookie notice) so you can see which pages and posts bring visitors. This needs a small code and CSP change. Ask when you're ready.

## Making money (later)

Paid plans are built (Pro and Premium through Paddle, plus an optional one-time **Job Search Pass**), and **free mode** keeps everything open until you decide to charge. Paddle goes live only once V2 is complete and tested.

When you have about 1,000 or more active users and real feedback:
- **Keep the core free forever:** building, ATS check and PDF download. It's your marketing.
- **Charge for extras:** more AI credits, premium templates, more tracked jobs and tailored CVs, batch tailoring. All of these limits are set in Admin › Plans.
- **Students in Bangladesh prefer one-time payments to subscriptions.** That's what the Job Search Pass is for: one payment for a plan for a set number of days (set in Admin › Plans). Test the price with a small group first.
- **Local payments matter.** Paddle handles cards and international users. Most Bangladeshi students pay with bKash or Nagad, so a local gateway (for example SSLCommerz, or bKash's own payment API) will convert much better locally. Look into it once people are asking to pay.
- Use **campaign codes for discounts** (for example `LAUNCH50`) and for university partnerships.

## Rules to protect the brand

- **Only real testimonials, reviews and numbers**, with permission.
- **No promises of jobs or interviews.**
- **Reply within a few hours.** Fast, friendly replies are your best advertising.
- **Never share a user's CV** in a post without clear written permission. Blur names and numbers even then.
- **Don't argue in comments.** Thank critics, fix the issue and reply "fixed, thanks!".
- **Consistent look:** use the teal and navy graphics in this kit so people start to recognise ResumeX in their feed.
