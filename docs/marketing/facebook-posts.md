### 10. Group version: tailoring your CV (value first)
**Graphic:** none

> A tip that helped a lot of my friends get more replies: **tailor your CV to every job ad.** It takes 10 minutes. 👇
>
> 1. Copy the job description and highlight the skills and tools it mentions more than once.
> 2. Make sure those exact words appear in your CV (only if they're true!), especially in your summary and skills.
> 3. Move your most relevant project or job to the top.
> 4. Change your headline to match the job title.
>
> Many companies use software that matches your CV against these keywords before a human reads it.
>
> Step-by-step guide: https://resumex.cc/guides/tailor-resume-to-job-description

### 12. CV vs resume
**Graphic:** `10-guide.png` (or none)

> CV or resume: what's the difference? 🤷
>
> 📄 **Resume:** 1–2 pages, tailored to one job. Used for most jobs in the US, Canada and at many companies.
> 📚 **CV (academic):** a full record of your academic life (research, publications, teaching). Used for universities, scholarships and research roles.
>
> In Bangladesh, the UK and much of Asia, people say "CV" for both, and they usually mean a 1–2 page resume.
>
> Full explanation 👉 https://resumex.cc/guides/cv-vs-resume
