# Outreach and reply templates

Copy, personalise (name, university, club) and send. A short personal message works better than a long formal one. Never send the same message to hundreds of people at once. Facebook limits accounts that do.

## Replies to common comments

**"Is it really free?"**
> Yes! Building your CV, the ATS check and the PDF download are all free, with no watermark. 😊

**"Is my data safe?"**
> Great question. Your CVs are private unless you choose to share them, passwords are securely hashed, and we never sell data. If you'd rather nothing is saved at all, use Private mode: https://resumex.cc/builder?private=1

**"Can I make a CV in Bangla?"**
> Right now the templates are designed for English CVs, which most employers in Bangladesh expect. Bangla support is on our list. Thanks for asking! 🙏

**"Can I track my applications?"** *(once the job-search tools are live)*
> Yes! Save each job to your board and follow it from saved to offer. You'll see deadlines and interviews, get reminders, and we keep the exact CV you sent. 👉 https://resumex.cc/applications

**"Does tailoring use AI? Will it make things up?"** *(once live)*
> No AI, and it never adds anything. It picks from what's already in your Career Profile and puts what the job asks for first. If the job wants a skill you haven't added, it simply tells you.

**"Is my biodata information safe?"** *(once live)*
> Biodata details (parents' names, addresses and so on) stay in that one CV only. They aren't saved to your profile, aren't sent to any AI and never appear on share links.

**"Does it work on mobile?"**
> Yes, it works on your phone. For the best editing experience a laptop is a bit easier, but you can do everything on mobile.

**"What is ATS?"**
> An ATS (Applicant Tracking System) is software companies use to collect and filter CVs. It reads your CV's text and matches it to the job. Our free checker shows you what it sees: https://resumex.cc/ats-checker

**"Which template should I use?"**
> For online job portals, choose one from the ATS-friendly collection (https://resumex.cc/templates/category/ats-friendly). If you're emailing or handing your CV to a person, a creative or two-column design is great too!

**"It's not working / I found a bug"**
> Sorry about that! Could you message us what you were doing and which device or browser you're on? We'll fix it fast. You can also email support@resumex.cc 🙏

**Negative comment**
> Thanks for the honest feedback. We're working on it! Could you message us the details so we can fix it for you?
