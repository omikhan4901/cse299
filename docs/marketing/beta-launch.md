# Beta launch: the first 80 students

A step-by-step plan for inviting the first 80 students, keeping them active for four weeks,
and learning from them. Everything here uses features that are already built.

## 1. Set up (30 minutes, the day before)

**Admin console › Credits & access**
- Who can sign up: **Only people with a campaign code**.
- Close sign-ups after: **90** accounts (80 students, you and a few spares).
- AI credits only after the email is verified: **on**.
- Payments: **Off** (pricing shows "Coming soon").
- AI monthly cap (AI costs tab): **$40**, alert at **80%**.

**Admin console › Campaigns › New campaign**
- Name: `Beta 1` · Code: keep the random one it suggests (like `BETA-7KQ2XM`), because
  short codes get guessed and passed around.
- Plan: **Pro** for **28 days** · Places: **80**.
- Custom credits: **40 a month**. The estimate should show about **$67 worst case** and
  **$17 typical**. The $40 monthly cap stops anything beyond that, so you can't lose more.
- Optional: an email domain (for example `northsouth.edu`) if you only invite one university.

**Admin console › Site**
- Beta tag: **on**. What's new: three short lines. Known issues: leave empty for now.
- Welcome note, for example: *"Thanks for testing ResumeX early! Start by bringing in your
  old CV, then add the jobs you're applying to. Tell us anything that feels off: the Beta
  tag at the top has a feedback button."*

**Admin console › Plans:** make sure V2 is on for everyone.

**Backups:** take the first one (`docs/backups.md`), so you have a copy from before the beta.

Then open `https://resumex.cc/join/<your code>` in a private window and check the invite
page shows the right plan, days and credits.

## 4. What to check every day (10 minutes)

1. **Overview:** active today and this week, sign-ups left, AI spend against the cap,
   feedback waiting, errors today.
2. **Bell** (top right): anything new? Spend alerts, storage, sign-ups nearly full, a burst
   from one network.
3. **Feedback:** reply to new ones (even "Thanks, fixing it this week").
4. **Feedback › Errors:** anything happening more than a couple of times? Note it for a fix.
5. **Sign-ups:** who joined and what they did first.

If AI spend is climbing fast: pause AI in AI costs until you've had a look (everything
else keeps working), and give the heaviest users (Overview › Top AI users) a smaller
allowance in Admin › Users. Changing the campaign only affects people who join after. If something is broken for everyone: Site ›
Maintenance mode on, fix, off.

## 5. After the beta

- Export the Google Form answers and the Feedback inbox; list the top 5 requests.
- Decide prices and turn on payments (Credits & access › Payments: Test first, then Live).
- Offer beta students a discount code for their first month (Paddle discounts).
- Keep the best feedback quotes (with permission) for the pricing page and posts.
